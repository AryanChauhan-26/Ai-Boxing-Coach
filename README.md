🥊 AI Coach for Boxing
An AI-powered boxing coach designed to help fighters analyze movement, improve technique, and optimize training through intelligent feedback and performance tracking.
The system uses AI/computer vision to analyze boxing movements and provide actionable feedback on form, speed, accuracy, footwork, and technique — bringing a virtual boxing coach to your training environment.

🚀 Features
🥊 Real-Time Movement Analysis
Analyze punches, stance, guard position, footwork, and body movement.
🤖 AI-Powered Coaching
Receive intelligent feedback to identify technical mistakes and areas for improvement.
🎯 Technique Analysis
Evaluate punches such as:
Jab
Cross
Hook
Uppercut
Combinations
⚡ Speed & Performance Tracking
Track punch speed, reaction time, movement efficiency, and overall performance.
📊 Performance Dashboard
Monitor progress over time and identify strengths and weaknesses.
🏋️ AI Workout Generation

Generate personalized boxing workouts based on performance and training goals.

📌 The Universal Line-Cutter Rule:

Archery Knowledge Matrix

🏹 9-Step Olympic Shot Cycle ▼
  1. Stance: Open 45° or square, feet shoulder-width.
  2. Grip & Hook: Deep hook, relaxed wrist, 45° knuckle line.
  3. Set & Mindset: Visualizing arrow path, steady breathing.
  4. Setup: Raise bow arm, align scapulae without hunching.
  5. Draw: Engage back latissimus & rhomboid muscles.
  6. Anchor: Solid index/jawbone bone-to-bone contact.
  7. Transfer & Aim: Transfer weight from arms to back tension.
  8. Expansion & Release: Steady pull through clicker; relaxed fingers.
  9. Follow-Through: Maintain bow arm stability for 2 seconds.
⚙️ Arrow Tuning Quick Rules ▼
  • Stiff Arrow: Impact left (RH archer) → Increase draw weight, increase point weight, or soften plunger.
  • Weak Arrow: Impact right (RH archer) → Decrease draw weight, decrease point weight, or stiffen plunger.
  • Nocking Point High: Bare shaft hits low → Lower nocking point.
  • Nocking Point Low: Bare shaft hits high → Raise nocking point.

Official Boxing Rules, 10-Point Scoring & Foul Protocol

Crystal-clear guide to how boxing rounds are scored, legal target areas, foul penalties, knockdowns, and standing 8-count rules.

🏆 The 10-Point Must Scoring System

Championship Scoring

Every round is scored independently by 3 ringside judges according to the 10-Point Must System:

10 - 9 Round: Close / Standard Round
Winner gets 10; Opponent gets 9
10 - 8 Round: Knockdown or Clear Dominance
1 Knockdown scored against opponent
10 - 7 Round: Multi-Knockdown Round
2+ Knockdowns in single round
10 - 10 Round: Even Round
Rarely awarded by pro judges
🥊 4 Official Judging Criteria:
  1. Clean Punching: Clean, landed strikes with knuckle part of glove.
  2. Effective Aggressiveness: Landing punches while moving forward.
  3. Ring Generalship: Controlling pace, distance, and ring center.
  4. Defense: Parrying, slipping, and blocking incoming attacks.

🎯 Legal Target Zones vs Illegal Strikes

Strike Legality
🟢 LEGAL TARGETS
  • ✓ Front of head & face
  • ✓ Sides of head & temples
  • ✓ Front of torso (chest, solar plexus)
  • ✓ Sides of body (ribs, liver)
  • ✓ All above the belt waistband line
🚫 ILLEGAL FOUL ZONES
  • ✕ Low blows (below belt line / groin)
  • ✕ Rabbit punch (back of head/neck)
  • ✕ Kidney punches (lower back spine)
  • ✕ Hitting with elbow, forearm, or head
  • ✕ Hitting an opponent while down
🚫 Low Blow Penalty

Up to 5 minutes recovery time awarded to victim. Point deduction for repeat fouls.

🚫 Clinching & Holding

Holding opponent's arms prevents action. Referee calls "Break!"—step back 1 full step.
